# Tata EV Intelligent Route & Charging Planner

An AI-powered navigation platform designed specifically for **Tata Electric Vehicles**, helping drivers find the **best possible route with optimal charging stations across India**.  
The goal is to eliminate range anxiety and make EV travel confident, predictable, and seamless.

---

## What This Platform Does

This application intelligently plans journeys for Tata EV owners by combining:
- Smart route selection
- Charging-aware navigation
- Interactive AI assistance

It is built to behave like a real driving companion rather than just a map.

---

## Core Capabilities

### Intelligent Route Planning
- Identifies the best route between source and destination
- Considers real EV constraints such as battery range and charging needs
- Minimizes risk of low-battery situations during long trips

### Charging-Aware Navigation
- Automatically identifies suitable charging stations along the route
- Helps decide **where to stop, how long to charge, and when to proceed**
- Supports inter-city and long-distance travel across India

### Interactive Navigation Experience
- Clear, map-based route visualization
- Seamless turn-by-turn style journey flow
- Designed to feel familiar and easy for everyday drivers

### AI-Driven Assistance
- Users can interact with the system conversationally
- AI helps plan trips, understand charging strategy, and answer journey-related questions
- Makes the platform adaptive and user-friendly rather than static

### India-First, Tata-Focused
- Built specifically for Indian driving conditions
- Optimized for Tata EV models and charging ecosystem
- Prioritizes practicality over theoretical routing

---

## Who This Is For

- Tata EV owners
- EV drivers planning long or unfamiliar routes
- Users concerned about charging availability and battery safety
- Anyone seeking a smarter, more confident EV navigation experience

---

## Vision

The long-term vision of this platform is to:
- Reduce EV range anxiety at scale
- Encourage adoption of electric mobility in India
- Provide a single intelligent system for routing, charging, and decision-making
- Set a new standard for EV navigation tailored to Indian roads
